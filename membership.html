Corporate Membership

Reach more than 16,000 landscape architecture professionals worldwide. Membership ranges from $999–$2,499 depending on company size.

Why Become a Corporate Member?

As a corporate member, you have the exclusive opportunity to meet landscape professionals personally through chapter events. The Corporate Member Program provides networking opportunities, brand exposure, and access to members-only professional resources.

Corporate Member Benefits Include:

  • Direct access to 16,000+ members through use of the ASLA USPS mailing list (up to 4 times/year)
  • ASLA corporate member logo use in your advertising and marketing materials
  • Opportunity to join up to three Professional Practice Networks (PPNs)
  • Access to quarterly industry economic and marketing insights
  • Quarterly recognition in a full-page LAM ad thanking all Corporate Members
  • Regular legislative and policy updates from ASLA Government Affairs
  • Access/participation/outreach with an ASLA chapter of your choosing
  • Complimentary subscriptions to LAM, LAND, The Landscape Report, The Dirt, and The Field
